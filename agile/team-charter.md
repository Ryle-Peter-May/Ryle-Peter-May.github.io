## Evidence

### Team purpose

**Objective**

The objective of this business is to provide affordable, reliable, and community-based security services to residents of Soshanguve, improving safety through rapid emergency response, local employment, and the use of a mobile alert system.

**Vision statement**

Our vision is to transform township safety across South Africa by building community-led security solutions that are accessible, effective, and empowering for all.

### Core values

| Value | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| **Accountability** | Every member takes ownership of their assigned tasks and meets agreed deadlines without needing to be reminded. |
| **Collaboration** | We work together and support one another rather than working in isolation. No one is left behind. |
| **Respect** | All opinions and ideas are heard and valued regardless of role or background. |
| **Transparency** | We are honest about our progress, challenges, and when we need help. No one hides problems from the group. |
| **Commitment** | We show up, contribute equally, and follow through on what we promise to the team. |

### Agile principles we follow

- **Individuals and interactions over processes and tools:** we prioritise open, honest communication within our team above rigid procedures.
- **Responding to change:** if our approach is not working, we adapt quickly rather than sticking to a plan that is failing.
- **Continuous improvement:** after each deliverable, we reflect on what went well and what to improve next time.
- **Delivering working results:** we focus on producing quality, complete work rather than just ticking boxes or meeting minimum requirements.

### Working agreements

**Communication method**

The team holds meetings on Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp, and face to face. MS Teams is mainly used for daily stand-up meetings, where we discuss tasks, progress and challenges. WhatsApp is used for updates, sharing documents, and reminders. Face-to-face sessions are used to discuss tasks in person.

**Frequency of meetings**

The daily stand-up is held Monday to Friday on MS Teams to check progress and challenges. WhatsApp is used for quick updates and as a fallback when MS Teams has connectivity issues. Face-to-face discussions are held when necessary to support effective collaboration.

## Reflection

The charter was our first piece of agreed structure. It set out who did what, the values we would hold ourselves to, and how we would communicate: a daily stand-up from Monday to Friday on MS Teams, with WhatsApp as the fallback.

The agreement that worked best was the daily stand-up. I kept a log of it every day, including weekends, and it gave me a reliable picture of progress and blockers across the first weeks. The value of transparency showed in the log too, where I recorded days when nothing was done instead of hiding them.

The part that did not hold up was the roles. Researcher, writer and designer suited the business-validation phase, but once we started building, every member took development stories, so the charter no longer described how we worked.

Next time I would treat the charter as a living document. I would revisit it at the start of each phase and update the roles and membership so that it matches what the team is actually doing.
